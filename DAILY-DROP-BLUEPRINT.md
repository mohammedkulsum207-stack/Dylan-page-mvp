# Daily Drop — Master Product & Business Blueprint

**Status:** Product blueprint and implementation roadmap  
**Repository:** `mohammedkulsum207-stack/Dylan-page-mvp`  
**Product direction:** A premium, creator-owned digital newsroom and media business.

## 1. Vision

Build Daily Drop into an independent digital media company with a professional newsroom, trusted journalism, an engaged reader community, multiple revenue streams, and clear owner control. The current repository is a mobile-first news MVP; this document defines the broader target product and the stages required to reach it.

## 2. Product principles and visual identity

- Premium, clean, responsive experience across mobile and desktop.
- White primary background.
- Black main headings.
- Blue subheadings, links, and key interface accents.
- Red reserved for breaking-news indicators and urgent alerts.
- Strong readability, accessible contrast, fast loading, and consistent layouts.
- Optional dark mode, without compromising the core visual identity.

## 3. Ownership and governance

The founder/owner has ultimate authority over the platform and business:
- Own and administer the platform and its configuration.
- Appoint, suspend, and remove staff.
- Grant, review, and revoke role permissions.
- Approve business strategy, partnerships, budgets, and financial decisions.
- Review publishing activity, security events, and audit logs.

Staff receive only the permissions needed for their assigned duties. Sensitive actions must be attributable to an authenticated account and recorded in an audit trail.

## 4. Five-stage product roadmap

### Stage 1 — Core platform and newsroom

**Owner control centre**
- Secure owner account and dashboard.
- Staff and role administration.
- Platform settings and operational oversight.
- Financial and strategic reporting.
- Audit logs for important actions.

**Newsroom operations**
- Staff accounts and role-based permissions for editors, journalists, reporters, photographers, and administrators.
- Story assignments, deadlines, editorial review, approval, scheduling, and publication.
- Internal newsroom communication and correction workflows.

**Publishing system**
- Article editor with drafts and revisions.
- Image and document uploads.
- Categories, tags, featured stories, and related articles.
- Breaking-news labels and scheduled publishing.
- Archive, corrections, and update history.

### Stage 2 — Readers and community

**Reader accounts**
- Registration, sign-in, and profiles.
- Followed topics and personalised feeds.
- Reading history, bookmarks, and account settings.

**Community**
- Comments and replies, reactions, and polls.
- Reader news tips and submissions.
- Reporting, moderation tools, and community guidelines.

**Notifications and newsletters**
- Breaking-news alerts and topic-based notifications.
- Daily and weekly newsletters.
- Email subscriptions and, when supported, mobile push notifications.

**Platforms**
- Premium responsive website.
- Android application.
- Plan for coordinated website and Android launch, subject to readiness and testing.

### Stage 3 — Revenue and commercial operations

**Membership**
- Proposed premium membership price: **US$60 per month**; validate willingness to pay before launch.
- Potential benefits: ad-free reading, exclusive investigations, special reports, interviews, premium newsletters, and early access.
- Clear free-versus-paid feature boundaries and transparent cancellation terms.

**Free reader experience**
- Standard news and breaking coverage.
- Standard videos, search, sharing, and engagement.
- Bookmarks, topic personalisation, polls, reader tips, notifications, and public newsletters.
- Advertising that has been reviewed and approved by Daily Drop.

**Advertising**
- Packages, rate cards, advertiser records, and campaign management.
- Banner, sponsored, and video placements.
- Scheduling, performance reports, contracts, and invoices.
- Advertisers submit campaign material but cannot publish directly. Daily Drop reviews, approves, schedules, and controls all advertising.

**Finance and partnerships**
- Track membership, advertising, sponsorship, and partnership income.
- Expense tracking, budgets, reconciliation, and profit-and-loss reporting.
- Corporate partnerships, sponsorships, brand collaborations, and institutional relationships.

### Stage 4 — Multimedia, intelligence, and distribution

- **Daily Drop Watch:** video news, interviews, documentaries, live broadcasts, and on-demand viewing.
- **Daily Drop Audio:** podcasts, interviews, audio articles, and daily briefings.
- **Daily Drop Live:** live coverage, events, interviews, and urgent updates.
- **Daily Drop Originals:** investigations, documentaries, special reports, and original interviews.
- **Daily Drop Intelligence:** research, analysis, business and economic reporting, data journalism, trend analysis, and fact-checking.
- **Distribution:** WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, email, feeds, and syndication, with editorial control over what is shared.

### Stage 5 — Business operations, security, and launch

**People operations**
- Staff records, recruitment, job applications, contracts, payroll, attendance, and performance management.
- Permission reviews and staff offboarding.

**Legal and editorial compliance**
- Privacy policy, terms of service, editorial standards, copyright procedures, correction policy, advertising disclosures, data-protection obligations, and complaints handling.
- Obtain appropriate legal review before public launch.

**Security**
- Strong authentication and role-based access.
- Two-factor authentication where available.
- Secure secrets handling, backups, audit trails, abuse prevention, monitoring, and incident response.
- Data minimisation and controlled access to reader information.

**Analytics**
- Audience and article performance.
- Subscription and advertising performance.
- Revenue, expenses, and operational dashboards.
- Staff and newsroom workflow metrics, with appropriate access controls.

**Launch readiness**
- Production website and Android app.
- Domain and Play developer account.
- App signing, security testing, privacy/legal review, real editorial content, support processes, and launch campaign.
- Do not represent prototype/demo features as production-ready until they are implemented and tested.

## 5. Editorial trust and safety

- Clearly distinguish verified reporting, opinion, sponsored content, and illustrative demo content.
- Provide source links and publication/update times where appropriate.
- Maintain visible correction and update records.
- Establish a fact-checking and escalation process for sensitive or urgent claims.
- Protect reader data and moderate user-generated content consistently.

## 6. Implementation priorities

The roadmap should be delivered incrementally rather than treating every proposed feature as already available.

1. Audit the existing MVP and preserve working functionality.
2. Establish the visual system and core navigation.
3. Build secure authentication, owner controls, and role permissions.
4. Complete editorial workflows and persistent publishing.
5. Add reader accounts, bookmarks, comments, and moderation.
6. Integrate notifications and newsletters.
7. Add payment and membership only after pricing and compliance validation.
8. Add advertising and finance workflows.
9. Expand multimedia, analytics, and distribution.
10. Complete security, legal, operational, and launch testing.

## 7. Current repository baseline and gaps

The current project README describes a mobile-first News Daddy MVP with Latest, Breaking, Live, and Trending feeds; story pages with source links and publication times; engagement metrics; visible corrections; a demo creator dashboard; responsive UI; and GitHub Pages deployment.

The README also states that its stories are illustrative demo content. Production accounts, push notifications, real publishing, live streaming, analytics, and moderation still need to be connected. These are baseline limitations to verify and address—not capabilities to assume are complete.

## 8. Decisions to validate before production

- Confirm the final public brand name and any relationship to the earlier News Daddy MVP branding.
- Validate the proposed US$60/month membership price with target readers.
- Define the first launch market, editorial coverage, languages, and payment methods.
- Confirm the legal entity, tax/accounting arrangements, privacy obligations, and editorial policies.
- Select production hosting, database, authentication, media storage, email, and notification providers.
- Define measurable launch criteria for reliability, security, accessibility, and editorial quality.

---

**Guiding rule:** Daily Drop should grow from a functioning news MVP into a trusted, secure, financially sustainable media company. Every new capability must have a clear owner, workflow, permission model, and tested production implementation.

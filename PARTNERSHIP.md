# News Daddy × This MVP

## The opportunity

This MVP is a starting point for News Daddy’s owned mobile news platform: breaking news, live coverage, verified sources, trending stories, and direct monetization.

Instead of relying entirely on TikTok, News Daddy would control its feed, notifications, audience data, sponsorships, and revenue.

## Partnership roles

| Area | MVP builder | News Daddy |
|---|---|---|
| Product, design, engineering | Lead | Input and approval |
| Infrastructure and operations | Lead | Input |
| Content and editorial voice | Support | Lead |
| Audience and brand | Support | Lead |
| Sponsorships and brand deals | Shared | Lead |
| Revenue and equity | Negotiated | Negotiated |

## Revenue streams

1. Sponsored posts and branded segments
2. In-app display and native ads
3. Sponsored trending placements
4. Premium subscriptions
5. Supporter tips and badges
6. Affiliate revenue with disclosure
7. Future licensing and syndication

## Proposed first step

Launch a branded pilot, measure downloads, daily active users, notification opt-ins, story completion, returning users, and sponsorship interest. Then negotiate a formal revenue-share and equity structure.
